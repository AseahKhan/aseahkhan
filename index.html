Aseah
Khan

I'm a New York City-based photographer drawn to the ordinary moments that go unnoticed — the way light cuts across a subway platform, a stranger mid-thought, a city corner that holds its breath.

My practice sits at the intersection of street photography and travel documentary, with a deep interest in the feeling of being both inside and outside a place at once. I shoot on film and digital, often returning to the same subjects across seasons.

I'm also a writer — my Substack bestfriendsonly pairs personal essays with my photo series. You can find more of my work on Instagram at _aseah_.
